Get Presence

  1. 1
    Open in Safari Tap the button above. Presence must be added from Safari; other browsers can't install it.
  2. 2
    Tap the Share icon It's the square with an upward arrow, at the bottom of Safari.
  3. 3
    Add to Home Screen Scroll down the menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. 4
    Confirm Tap Add. Presence appears on your home screen like any app.
  1. 1

    Install & open Tap Install, then open Presence and sign in with your portal.
