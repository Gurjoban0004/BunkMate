BUILT DIFFERENTLY

Engineered around college reality.

Most apps ask you to tap a button every time you attend a class. Presence automates the entire loop.

Official Portal Truth

Your attendance is pulled directly from your university’s ERP records. Teachers submit marks; Presence reflects them within hours. What you see is what the registrar sees.

Skip & Recovery Math

Never guess how many lectures you can miss. If you are safe, Presence shows your exact skip buffer. If you fall below 75%, it calculates the exact consecutive classes needed to recover.

One-Time Sign In

Authenticate once with your student roll number. Your credentials communicate directly with your college. No recurring CAPTCHA walls and no surprise session timeouts.

Hermes Native Speed

Compiled with Hermes bytecode for instant startup. The app reads from your phone’s local cache first, so you can check your schedule inside basement classrooms with zero signal.
