# Product

## Register

product

## Users

College students on Android and iOS who track class attendance daily. They check the app before deciding whether to attend a lecture — often in under 30 seconds, between classes or on the way in. They care deeply about the exact number: can I skip this or not? They are not passive consumers; they are calculating.

## Product Purpose

Presence is an offline-first attendance tracker that gives students precise, trustworthy answers to two questions: "Is it safe to skip today?" and "How do I recover if I'm in danger?" It replaces manual percentage math and spreadsheet anxiety with a clean, fast, always-available tool. Success looks like a student opening the app, getting a confident answer, and closing it — in under 10 seconds.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The app is on the student's side, but it never lies to them. It should feel like a very smart, composed friend who gives you the honest answer — not a wellness app cheering you on, not an institutional system judging you.

## Anti-references

- **Generic school LMS (Moodle/Canvas):** Institutional, clunky, built for administrators not students. Presence should feel the opposite — personal and immediate.
- **Habit-tracker wellness apps:** Pastel streaks, motivational quotes, dopamine-loop design. Presence is about real numbers, not encouragement theater.
- **Corporate SaaS dashboards:** Dense enterprise nav, data tables built for analysts. Presence is mobile-first and task-first — one answer per screen.
- **Crypto/fintech apps:** Dark OLED everything, neon accents, overwhelming data density. Presence should be readable and calm even when the situation is urgent.

## Design Principles

1. **The number is the hero.** Every screen's job is to surface the critical number fast — percentage, bunks remaining, classes to recover. Decoration that competes with the number is waste.
2. **Calm under pressure.** The interface stays composed even when the data is urgent. Danger is conveyed through semantic color and clear labels, not alarm-style UI patterns.
3. **Earn the skip.** The Planner's job is honest math, not permission-giving. The app helps the student understand the real situation; it doesn't soften the numbers or celebrate risky choices.
4. **The tool disappears.** Interactions should be near-instant. No unnecessary confirmation dialogs, no animation that makes the user wait for information they already need.
5. **Consistency over cleverness.** The same affordance in two places should look identical. Reliability builds trust; surprise erodes it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA as the floor. Both light and dark variants of every palette must meet 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and UI components. The app is checked frequently in varied ambient light (outdoor, dim dorm rooms) — contrast is non-negotiable. Reduced motion support is required for all transitions.
